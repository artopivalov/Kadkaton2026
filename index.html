Controls and prototype rules

Move with the joystick, WASD or arrow keys. Safe never fires. Normal: hold the joystick or play area to charge, even while standing still; release to cast a fireball. More charge means a larger fireball, more damage and longer range. Quick taps produce tiny, almost harmless sparks. Ice casts a spread of icicles; Lightning casts an instant line. Special: Fireball creates a fire sphere around you; Ice sends a cold wave forward. Both require full charge. Lightning moves a targeting marker farther as you hold; release at any charge to strike that point from the sky. On keyboard, release the last movement key to cast. Tap the wand slot to drop it; move close with an empty slot to pick it up. Swipe the mode slider up or down: Safe at the bottom, Normal in the middle, Special at the top. Arrow keys also work when the slider is focused. Changing mode cancels charge. Balance values are provisional. Air pushes targets; Earth casts a ricocheting boulder or a temporary solid wall. Test Wand casts harmless sparks or a harmless sphere. In the playground, walk up to a pedestal to swap your equipped wand. In the lobby, portals are not available yet. Self-damage and mana rules are not defined yet.
