Controls and prototype rules

Move with the joystick, WASD or arrow keys. Safe never fires. Normal: hold a direction to move and charge; release to cast a fireball. Special: hold longer until fully charged, then release to cast a fire sphere around the wizard. Releasing early cancels Special. On keyboard, release the last movement key to cast. Tap the wand slot to drop it; move close with an empty slot to pick it up. Swipe the mode slider up or down: Safe at the bottom, Normal in the middle, Special at the top. Arrow keys also work when the slider is focused. Changing mode cancels charge. Release gestures and balance values are provisional. Self-damage and mana rules are not defined yet.
