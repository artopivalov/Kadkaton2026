    Controls and prototype rules

    Move with the joystick, WASD or arrow keys. Safe never fires. Normal: hold the joystick or play area to charge, even while standing still; release to cast a fireball. More charge means a larger fireball, more damage and longer range. Quick taps produce tiny, almost harmless sparks. Ice casts a spread of icicles; Lightning casts an instant line. Special: Fireball creates a fire sphere around you; Ice sends a cold wave forward. Both require full charge. Lightning moves a targeting marker farther as you hold; release at any charge to strike that point from the sky. On keyboard, release the last movement key to cast. Tap the wand slot to drop it; move close with an empty slot to pick it up. Swipe the mode slider up or down: Safe at the bottom, Normal in the middle, Special at the top. Arrow keys also work when the slider is focused. Changing mode cancels charge. Balance values are provisional. Air pushes targets; Earth casts a ricocheting boulder or a temporary solid wall. Test Wand casts harmless sparks or a harmless sphere. In the playground, walk up to a pedestal to swap your equipped wand. Choose Item rarity and walk up to the rune station to get a random rune, replacing the equipped one. Nature roots or creates slowing vines. Gravity pulls or creates a damaging well. Light throws a returning disc or a wall that blocks projectiles but allows movement. Crystal shoots a guided splitting shard or places a trap. In the lobby, enter a location portal together. Clear combat arenas or activate numbered puzzle plates in order; approach the unlocked chest for two shared items. Combat starts with a random battle wand. Runes modify seven shared wand stats; tap the rune slot to drop one. Red areas warn of boss attacks. Special spells use mana, which regenerates. Friendly fire deals half damage. Use the right slider to zoom. In the generation viewer, the joystick and keyboard move a free camera through walls; Regenerate recreates the selected seed and location.
